Mexico vs South Africa:
The World Cup Opens

The 2010 Callback

Mexico and South Africa opened the 2010 World Cup on African soil. Now, 16 years later, they meet again — this time at the spiritual home of Mexican football. The Azteca roars. The same fixture. A different continent. A different decade.

What South Africa Carries

Bafana Bafana are here representing 10 African nations and 200 million absent Nigerian eyes. The opening match of a tournament isn't just a game. It's a declaration. South Africa know it.

Key Battle

Hirving Lozano vs the South Africa press. Mexico's wing play will define whether they can control the opening match rhythm. South Africa will press high and early.
